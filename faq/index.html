Questions and answers

What is Cosy Cat Games?

A small collection of calm, cat-themed games I made for my own kids, with the help of AI. They are short, gentle games like cooking, matching and sorting, made to feel cosy rather than exciting.

Is it really free of ads and tracking?

Yes. There are no ads, no pop-ups, no “free gifts”, no chat and no analytics. Nothing tracks what your child does. The only outside service the site loads is Google Fonts, for its lettering.

Does it cost anything?

Right now every game is free to play.

Who are the games for?

The current games suit kids aged about 5 to 10. Younger kids often enjoy them with a grown-up nearby. Games for toddlers are on the way.

How do I put it on my home screen?

Adding it to your home screen makes it open full screen like an app, and it works offline.

  • iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap Share (on newer iPhones it's inside the ••• menu), then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to home screen or Install app.
  • Computer: in Chrome or Edge, use the install icon in the address bar or the menu.
Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the games have been opened with an internet connection, they keep working without one, which is handy in the car or on a plane.

Where is my child's progress saved?

On the device they are playing on, and nowhere else. Players are just a first name and an animal picture.

Clearing your browser's data for this site removes saved players and progress, so it's worth skipping this site if you clear your history.

What do you store about my kids?

Nothing on our side. Names, scores and finished dishes stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Do the games try to make kids play longer?

No. There are no streaks, daily rewards, timers or “come back” messages. A game can be put down at any point and nothing is lost.
