Questions and answers

What is Cosy Cat Games?

A small collection of calm, cat-themed games I made for my own kids, with the help of AI. They are short, gentle games like cooking, matching, sorting, colouring and growing plants, made to feel cosy rather than exciting.

Is it really free of ads and tracking?

Yes. There are no ads, no pop-ups, no “free gifts” and no chats or messages. Nothing follows your child around the web or builds a profile of them.

We count visits, and which games, levels, pictures and dishes get picked, so we can see what's most popular. We use Vercel Web Analytics (from our web host) and Umami (on our own analytics server). Neither uses cookies, and neither ever gets nicknames, avatars or anything that identifies you or your child.

Does it cost anything?

Not right now. Every game is free for a limited time while we test them, fix bugs and add more games.

Who are the games for?

Kids from toddlers to tweens. Each game has a suggested age, but it's only a suggestion: any game can be played by anyone, at any age. Toddler mode shows just the toddler games, with big buttons and no reading.

How do I put it on my home screen?

Adding it to your home screen makes it open full screen like an app, and it works offline.

  • iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap Share (on newer iPhones it's inside the ••• menu), then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to home screen or Install app.
  • Computer: in Chrome or Edge, use the install icon in the address bar or the menu.
Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the games have been opened with an internet connection, they keep working without one, which is handy in the car or on a plane.

Where is my child's progress saved?

On the device they play on, and nowhere else. Each device has one player: a made-up nickname and a cat avatar.

To keep progress safe, add the games to your home screen. Safari on iPhone and iPad can clear a website's saved data if it isn't opened for a week, but it leaves home screen apps alone. Clearing your browser's data for this site also removes progress.

Can brothers and sisters share a device?

For now each device has one player, so kids sharing a tablet share one name and one set of progress. Separate players will be available in the paid version.

How do I report a bug or suggest a game?

Use the feedback form. Bug reports from real families are the most helpful thing right now.

What do you store about my kids?

Nothing on our side. Nicknames, cat avatars, progress and the things they make stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. Our counts only show which pages, games and choices are picked, not who picked them.

Do the games try to make kids play longer?

No. There are no streaks, daily rewards, countdowns or “come back” messages. A game can be put down at any point and nothing is lost.

A couple of games have an optional Challenge mode for kids who like a bit of a rush, such as cat customers who won’t wait forever in Cat Café. It’s always their choice, and the relaxed mode is right next to it.

Do kids have to unlock things?

No. Every cat, hat and decoration is there from the start. There’s nothing to unlock, collect or buy. The plants, pictures and dishes in Cosy Home are simply the ones your child made in the other games.
