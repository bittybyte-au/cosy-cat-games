About Cosy Cat Games

Cosy Cat Games is a little collection of calm, colourful games for kids, made at home by a family.

The games are designed with the help of a young game designer, who tests every change and sends in plenty of feedback.

Our promise

  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • No pop-ups
  • No chat with strangers

The games

  • Cosy Kitchen: chop, mix and cook sushi hand rolls, pancakes and burgers, then decorate the plate.
  • Kitty Pop: a gentle matching game where you line up cats to pop them.

More games and recipes are on the way.

Your child's privacy

Players are just a first name and an animal picture. They are saved only on the device being played on, along with scores and finished dishes. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

We don't use analytics or advertising cookies. The only outside service the site loads is Google Fonts, for its lettering.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Add it to your Home Screen so it opens like an app. On iPhone or iPad, tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open Chrome's menu and choose Add to Home screen.
  • It works offline once it has been opened with an internet connection.
  • Clearing your browser's data for this site removes saved players and progress.
