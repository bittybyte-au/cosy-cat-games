About Cosy Cat Games

Constantly being pestered to install new apps? Worried about tracking, identifiers and inappropriate ads? Me too.

With the help of AI, I created a collection of simple games for my cat-obsessed kids. They’re designed to satisfy the urge to play, while staying free of the now seemingly mandatory dark patterns that have crept into nearly every kids’ gaming app: retention design tricks built to keep children on screens longer, keep them coming back and keep them buying.

Free gifts every time you log in. Paying to upgrade to the latest tools or accessories. Ads that promote yet more games. Streaks and countdowns that make a day off feel like losing something.

Worry-free games for parents and kids.

Every game is free while we’re still building and testing. If something breaks or you have an idea, tell us: your feedback shapes what comes next.

QA testing provided by my three kids.

Our promise

  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • No pop-ups
  • No chats or messages

Your child's privacy

Players are just a made-up nickname and a cat avatar. They are saved only on the device being played on, along with their progress and the things they make, like dishes, plants and pictures. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics, from our web host, to see which games are most popular. It uses no cookies and stores nothing that identifies you or your child. The only other time anything leaves your device is if you choose to send us feedback.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Add it to your Home Screen so it opens like an app. On iPhone or iPad, tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open Chrome's menu and choose Add to Home screen.
  • It works offline once it has been opened with an internet connection.
  • Clearing your browser's data for this site removes saved players and progress.
